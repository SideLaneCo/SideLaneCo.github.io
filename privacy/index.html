Privacy Policy

Effective August 4, 2026

Side Lane Co is an independent small business selling digital templates, guides, and print-on-demand products through Etsy, Gumroad, and Payhip. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used, including for our Pinterest app integration used to publish pins for our own shop.

What we collect

When you buy a product from us, the marketplace you purchased through (Etsy, Gumroad, or Payhip) handles your payment and order information under its own privacy policy. We don't separately collect or store your payment details.

If you create an account on RehabRunway, a separate app we also run, we store your email address and a securely hashed password to run your account, plus billing status if you subscribe. We don't sell or share this data with third parties.

Pinterest integration

Our Pinterest app connects only to our own business Pinterest account. It is used to publish and manage pins linking to our own product listings. It does not access, read, or store data belonging to any other Pinterest user. Access tokens are stored securely and are never shared with third parties.

Cookies

Our RehabRunway app uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. This page doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-party services

We use Etsy, Printify, Printful, Gumroad, Payhip, Pinterest, and Stripe to run parts of this business. Each of these has its own privacy policy governing data they process on their platforms.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to sidelaneco@gmail.com.
